SP5LOT sondehub rotator: third-party components
================================================

The program (sondehub_rotator.exe) is freeware by SP5LOT (LICENSE.txt). It contains the components
below, compiled into the exe. Their licences are reproduced in full.

  Dear ImGui            MIT                     https://github.com/ocornut/imgui
  GLFW 3.4              zlib/libpng             https://www.glfw.org
  JSON for Modern C++   MIT                     https://github.com/nlohmann/json (v3.11.3)
  zlib 1.3.1            zlib                    https://zlib.net
  MinGW-w64 winpthreads MIT and BSD-3-Clause    https://www.mingw-w64.org
  GCC runtime           GPL v3 with the GCC Runtime Library Exception (no notice required)
  Natural Earth         public domain           https://www.naturalearthdata.com (map outlines and cities)

Data used at run time (not included in the program): SondeHub (https://sondehub.org), APRS-IS
(https://www.aprs2.net) and findu.com.
